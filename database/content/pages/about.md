**Listee helps local shops get found, take orders and keep their customers coming back.**

## For customers

- Find restaurants and shops near you, see their menu, offers and reviews.
- Order for pickup, dine-in at your table, or home delivery — pay at the counter or online.
- Spin for rewards, collect coins and get birthday treats from the shops you love.

## For shops

- Your own page and QR codes for tables and the counter.
- Orders from the app, the table and the counter in one place, with kitchen tickets and Bluetooth bill printing.
- GST bills, staff logins with roles, attendance, day-end cash report and owner alerts.
- Offers, coupons, combos, loyalty and WhatsApp messages that bring customers back.
- A simple monthly, quarterly or yearly plan — no commission on your orders.

Questions? Write to **support@listee.app**.
