We are happy to help.

## Customers

For a problem with an order, contact the shop first — its phone number is on its Listee page. If it is not sorted out, write to us with your order token.

## Shops

For help with your listing, plan, billing, GST bills, staff or printing, write to us with your shop name and registered mobile number.

## Write to us

**Email:** support@listee.app

We reply within 2 working days. For privacy or data requests, see our [Privacy Policy](/p/privacy); to close your account, see [Delete account](/p/delete-account).
