You can delete your Listee account and its personal data at any time. This works for the **Listee** customer app and the **Listee Business** app.

## Delete from the app (fastest)

**Listee (customer app):** Profile → **Delete account** → type DELETE → confirm.

**Listee Business (shop owners):** More → **Delete account** → type DELETE → confirm.

**Website:** sign in at listee.app → Profile → **Delete account**.

The account is closed straight away and you are signed out on every device.

## Can't open the app?

Email **support@listee.app** from your registered email, or write from anywhere with your registered mobile number, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm it is you and delete the account within 7 days.

## What is deleted

- Your name, mobile number, email, PIN, photo, birthday and the people you added.
- Your notification tokens and sign-in sessions.
- Your favourites, coins and unused rewards.
- **For shop owners:** the shop is taken off Listee at once, its staff logins are switched off, and its menu, photos and staff records (including attendance selfies) are deleted within 30 days.

## What is kept, and why

- **Bills, invoices and payment records** are kept for up to 8 years because Indian tax law requires it. They no longer carry your name.
- **Reviews** you wrote stay on the shop's page as "Deleted user".
- Records we must keep to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation.

Everything else is deleted or anonymised within 30 days.

Staff of a shop do not have their own account to delete — ask the shop owner to remove you, or write to us.
