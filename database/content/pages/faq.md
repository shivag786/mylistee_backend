## For customers

**Is Listee free for customers?**
Yes. You pay only for what you order from a shop. Online payments may carry a small convenience fee, shown before you pay.

**How do I pay?**
At the shop counter in cash or UPI, or online in the app where the shop allows it.

**Where is my GST bill?**
Open the order → **View bill**. If the shop is GST-registered you get a tax invoice with its GSTIN, CGST and SGST.

**What are coins?**
Loyalty points from a shop. Use them as a discount at that shop where accepted. They have no cash value.

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile → Delete account. See [Delete account](/p/delete-account).

## For shops

**Do you take commission on orders?**
No. Shops pay a simple plan — monthly, quarterly or yearly. Online payments carry only the payment gateway's fee.

**Can my staff use the app?**
Yes. Add staff with a role (manager, cashier, waiter, kitchen). They sign in with your shop code, their mobile and a PIN, and see only what their role allows.

**Can I print GST bills?**
Yes. Turn on GST billing, add your GSTIN, and connect a Bluetooth thermal printer.

**Need more help?**
Write to info@listee.org.
