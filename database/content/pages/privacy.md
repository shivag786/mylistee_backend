**In short:** we collect what we need to sign you in, show you shops near you, pass your orders to the shop and run rewards. We do not sell your personal data and we do not run third-party ad tracking. Card, UPI and bank details go to Razorpay, never to us. You can delete your account from the app at any time.

This policy covers the Listee website, the **Listee** customer app and the **Listee Business** app for shop owners and their staff.

## 1. Who we are

Listee ("we", "us") operates the Listee platform and is the data fiduciary for personal data processed through it under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

A shop you order from is separately responsible for what it does with the details it receives through Listee, such as your name and phone number on an order.

## 2. What we collect

**Customer account.** Your name and mobile number, a PIN you choose (stored hashed), or — if you use Google Sign-In — your name, email address and profile photo from Google. Optionally your birthday, anniversary, gender and the names of people you celebrate, if you add them for birthday offers.

**Business owner account.** Your name, mobile number, email address and a PIN (stored hashed). Your shop's name, address and map pin, opening hours, phone, photos, menu, prices, tables, offers, and — if you add them — GSTIN, legal name and FSSAI number for bills.

**Staff of a shop.** The owner adds staff with a name, mobile number, role and PIN. If the shop uses attendance, we store check-in and check-out times, a **selfie photo** taken at check-in and out, and the **phone's location** at that moment, to confirm the person was at the shop. These are visible to the shop owner only.

**When you use Listee.** Shops you open, scan, favourite or visit; spins and rewards; your coin balance and its history; orders (items, amounts, table or delivery address, status); reviews; notification settings.

**Payments.** For online payments, Razorpay returns to us only the payment identifiers, amount, status and method type (e.g. "UPI"). We never receive your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or bank login.

**Device and technical data.** IP address, device model and app version, and a push-notification token if you allow notifications. Admin actions are written to an audit log.

## 3. App permissions and why we ask

| Permission | App | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Customer app | To show shops near you and distances. Only while you use the app; never in the background. |
| Location | Business app | To pin your shop on the map, and for staff attendance check-in at the shop. |
| Camera | Customer app | To scan a shop's QR code. |
| Camera | Business app | Shop and dish photos, and the staff check-in selfie. |
| Microphone | Customer app | Voice search, only while you hold the mic button. Audio is turned into text on your device and not stored by us. |
| Notifications | Both apps | Order updates, new-order alerts for shops, and offers (offers can be switched off). |
| Bluetooth | Business app | To print bills and kitchen tickets on a Bluetooth printer. |
| Photos / storage | Business app | To pick images from your gallery. |

You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone's settings; the related feature simply stops working.

## 4. Why we use your data

- To run your account and the service — sign-in, showing shops, passing orders, tracking coins and rewards.
- To take payments and issue bills and invoices, and to meet tax and accounting law.
- To send notifications about your orders, rewards and account. Offers and broadcasts only with your consent.
- To keep the platform safe — detecting fake accounts, reward abuse and fake reviews.
- To improve Listee using aggregated statistics that do not identify you.

We do not make automated decisions about you that have legal effects, and we do not profile you for advertising.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs, and we never sell personal data.

- **The shop you order from or visit** — your name, phone, order and delivery details, so it can serve you.
- **Google (Firebase)** — sign-in and push notifications.
- **Razorpay** — online payments for orders and plans.
- **WhatsApp Business (Meta)** — order and offer messages, if the shop or Listee sends them and you have given your number.
- **AI providers** — for owner tools such as offer suggestions and the assistant, we send shop data (menu, aggregate sales figures). No customer personal data.
- **Hosting providers** — who store and serve the platform under contract.
- **Authorities** — where the law requires it.

Some providers process data outside India under their own data-protection commitments.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Account and profile: while your account is open.
- Orders, coins and reviews: while your account is open.
- Bills, invoices and payment records: up to 8 years, as Indian tax law requires — these remain (without your name) after account deletion.
- Staff attendance selfies: as long as the shop keeps the staff record; deleted when the owner removes the staff member or closes the shop.
- Audit logs: up to 3 years.

When you delete your account, your name, phone, email and profile are removed immediately and the rest is deleted or anonymised within 30 days, except what the law makes us keep.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, withdraw consent (for example for notifications), and nominate someone to act for you. You can delete your account yourself — see [Delete account](/p/delete-account). For anything else, write to **info@listee.org** from your registered number or email; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 8. Security

Sign-in uses tokens over HTTPS, PINs are stored hashed, and access to production data is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach is likely to affect you we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

## 9. Children

Listee is not meant for children under 18 without a parent's supervision. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.
