**In short:** a food or goods order is refunded by the shop; a Listee plan payment is refunded by us in the cases below. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 working days.

## 1. Orders you pay online

When you pay for an order online, the payment is collected through Razorpay for the shop.

- **Shop cancels or rejects your order** — the amount you paid online is refunded in full, automatically.
- **Order cancelled before the shop accepts it** — refunded in full.
- **After the shop has accepted or started preparing** — cancellation and refund are at the shop's discretion.
- **Wrong, missing or bad item** — tell the shop first (its number is on its Listee page). If it is not resolved, write to support@listee.app with your order token within 48 hours; we review it with the shop.

Orders paid in cash or at the shop's own terminal are refunded by the shop directly.

## 2. Coins and rewards

Coins are loyalty points, not money — they cannot be refunded or turned into cash. Coins used on a cancelled order are returned to your balance.

## 3. Listee plans and modules (for shops)

Plans are paid monthly, quarterly or yearly. You can stop at any time; your paid features run until the end of the period, then the shop moves to the free plan. Nothing is deleted.

We refund a plan payment when:

- it is your **first paid plan** and you ask within **7 days**;
- you were **charged twice** for the same period;
- money was taken but the **plan did not activate**;
- a **fault on our side** stopped the service and we could not fix it (pro-rata);
- we **end your plan without cause** (pro-rata for the unused period).

We do not refund a period already used, unused features, or an account suspended for breaking our [Terms](/p/terms). Per-use module fees (per order or per staff) are not refundable once the use has happened.

## 4. How to ask

Email **support@listee.app** from your registered number or email with your shop name, the invoice or order number, the date and amount, and what went wrong.

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Decision | within 5 working days |
| Money back after approval | within 10 working days, to the original payment method |

A failed payment captures no money. If your bank shows a debit for a failed attempt, it is usually released by the bank within 5–7 working days; if not, send us the payment reference.
