## 1. What Listee is

Listee is a platform where local businesses list their shop, menu, offers and rewards, and take orders. We are an intermediary under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and a marketplace e-commerce entity under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

The business is the seller. Every product, price, tax, offer, reward and delivery is the responsibility of the business that listed it, and your purchase contract is with that business.

## 2. Accounts

## 3. Orders and payment

- When you place an order, Listee passes it to the shop and shows you its status. The shop may accept, change or cancel it (for example if an item is unavailable).
- You may pay the shop in cash or at its counter, or pay online where the shop allows it. Online payments are processed by Razorpay. A small convenience fee may be added to online payments; it is shown before you pay.
- Prices, GST and service charges are set by the shop and shown on your bill. Where a shop bills GST, the tax invoice is issued by that shop under its GSTIN.
- Problems with an order (wrong, late, missing, quality) go to the shop first. We help you reach them and act on repeated complaints. Refunds for online-paid orders are covered in the [Refund policy](/p/refund).

## 4. Rewards, spins and coins

- Rewards come from the shops. A spin draws from offers the shop has made live; you redeem what you win at that shop, on its terms, before it expires.
- Coins are loyalty points, not money. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn or transferred, and can be used only as a discount where accepted.
- Rewards or coins gained by error, bug or manipulation may be reversed and the account suspended.
- If a shop leaves Listee, its unused rewards and coins can no longer be used.

- honour every offer, combo and reward you publish while it is live;
- upload only content you have the right to use;

You keep ownership of your content and give us a licence to host and show it in Listee and in Listee promotion while your listing is live.
